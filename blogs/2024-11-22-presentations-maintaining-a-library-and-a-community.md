<p>The main thesis of this talk is that maintainers do a <em>lot</em> more than just write features and fix bugs, and that really most of what we do involves thinking about and interacting with the users of our library. That includes answering support questions, writing docs, thinking about how versioning and backwards compatibility will impact people, and a lot more.</p>

<p>Hopefully this proves useful to other maintainers, and peels back the curtain on what it's like to maintain a widely-used library.</p>
